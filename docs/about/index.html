  • 2016 to 2020surfed hurricane matthew, dorian and isaias. the storms are the best waves we get. i don't recommend it, and i still go.
  • 2019graduated saint andrew's school, boca raton.
  • collegestarted out the back. a surf and apparel brand for florida surfers who are too busy surfing to care what anyone thinks.
  • 2024surfed hurricane milton, told my mom first, filmed her reaction. 1.8 million views on tiktok and a feature in people magazine.
  • 2024 to 2025built the out the back van and the warehouse in boca. hosted the warehouse parties. put the brand on retail walls.

destination x

the tv show

destination x is a travel competition on nbc and peacock hosted by jeffrey dean morgan. you ride a blacked-out bus across europe, get fed clues and decoys, and drop an x on a map. farthest from the real spot goes home. i was on season 2 as the surf guy from boca who runs a brand. ask me about it on a call.

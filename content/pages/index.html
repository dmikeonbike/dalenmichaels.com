florida is wave-starved. you learn to surf anything and make the most of nothing. that attitude turned into everything that came after.

2016 → 2024

02

matthew, dorian, isaias, milton. the milton video went everywhere and people magazine ran the story. i still tell you not to do it.

03

started out the back at 20. from a bedroom.

a surf brand for florida surfers. blank hoodies first, then a van, a warehouse, a 30-creator team, and the brand on retail walls.

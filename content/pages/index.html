i make relatable videos you don't realize are ads. i run a surf brand out of boca raton. and i teach people how to build a life they're proud of.

2014 · first wave

caught my first wave at 13. surfing florida for real at 15.

florida is wave-starved. you learn to surf anything and make the most of nothing. ten years later that attitude is the whole brand.

2016 → 2024 · matthew, dorian, isaias, milton

surfed the hurricanes. told mom first.

the milton video went everywhere and people magazine ran the story. i still tell you not to do it.

april 2021 · age 20 · usc

started out the back from a college dorm.

a surf brand for florida surfers. printing tees in a usc dorm first. then a van, a warehouse, a 30-creator team, and the brand on retail walls.

college

built an influencer agency before i was an influencer.

i ran creators for brands first. that's why i represent myself, why my turnaround is fast, and why i already know what you need before you send the brief.

2024 → 2025

built the van. built the warehouse.

shop on wheels, content studio, home on the road. then a warehouse in boca for inventory, shipping, shoots, and the parties.

35 countries · destination x · nbc / peacock

35 countries. then nbc called.

i brought the brand to machu picchu, rio, and everywhere in between. destination x cast me because of how far i'd been. a blacked-out bus across europe and a map room was the easy part.

now

10 million people a month watch what i make.

with my mom, my dog, my friends, my brand and the ocean in the frame. this is where you come in.

privacy policy

last updated october 30, 2026.

this policy covers dalenmichaels.com and the dalen edit bay content tools, run by dalen michaels in boca raton, florida.

what we collect

on the site: nothing beyond standard analytics (page views, device type, country). if you fill in a form, we keep what you typed so we can reply.

in the tools: dalen edit bay connects only to dalen's own social media accounts. when it connects through a platform api (instagram, tiktok, youtube, linkedin) it stores an access token, the account id and username, and the videos and captions it posts. it reads post stats and comments on dalen's own posts so dalen can reply. it does not collect data about any other user.

how we use it

to post dalen's videos, read how they did, and reply to comments. nothing else. no selling, no sharing with advertisers.

where it lives

tokens live in a private file in dalen's own cloud storage. videos live in dalen's dropbox. nothing is stored on this website.

third parties

instagram and facebook (meta), tiktok, youtube (google), linkedin, dropbox, notion. each has its own privacy policy. we only use their official apis.

deleting your data

if you connected an account and want it removed, revoke the app in that platform's settings or email management.dalen@gmail.com and the token and any stored data are deleted within 7 days.

cookies

only what analytics needs. no ad tracking.

the story

who is dalen

i'm 25, from boca raton, surfing since 14. i run a surf brand called out the back, i make videos that reach about 10 million people a month, and i was on season 2 of destination x on nbc and peacock. i train jiu jitsu, i have a dog named dude, and i give god the glory for all of it.

timeline

how we got here

  • 2014started surfing in boca raton at 14. florida is wave-starved, so you learn to surf anything and make the most of nothing.
  • 2016 to 2020surfed hurricane matthew, dorian and isaias. the storms are the best waves we get. i don't recommend it, and i still go.
  • 2019graduated saint andrew's school, boca raton.
  • april 2021started out the back at 20. a surf and apparel brand for florida surfers who are too busy surfing to care what anyone thinks.
  • collegestarted an influencer agency and ran creators for brands. that's why i represent myself today and why brands say i'm easy to work with.
  • 2024surfed hurricane milton, told my mom first, filmed her reaction. millions of views and a feature in people magazine.
  • 2024 to 2025built the out the back van and the warehouse in boca. hosted the warehouse parties. put the brand on retail walls.
  • 2025 to 2026cast on destination x season 2 (nbc / peacock). a blacked-out bus across europe with jeffrey dean morgan asking where you think you are.
  • 2026usc marshall grad. 10 million views a month across instagram and tiktok. paid partnerships with native, vita coco, bumble, pura vida and more.
  • nowmentoring a few people at a time, speaking, surfing every swell, and building out the back into the florida surf brand.
out the back

the brand

florida-made surfwear. hoodies, tees, hats, bikinis, and a van that shows up at the beach. paid media, a 30-creator affiliate team, wholesale accounts, and a warehouse full of boxes i packed myself. outheback.com

destination x

the tv show

destination x is a travel competition on nbc and peacock hosted by jeffrey dean morgan. you ride a blacked-out bus across europe, get fed clues and decoys, and drop an x on a map. farthest from the real spot goes home. i was on season 2 as the surf guy from boca who runs a brand. ask me about it on a call.
